Privacy Policy

BMS Battery Monitor for iPhone · Effective 17 November 2026 · updated for in-app purchases

BMS Battery Monitor ("the app") shows and records readings from your battery's management board (BMS) over Bluetooth. This policy explains what the app does with data. In short: the app does not collect any personal data, and nothing you record leaves your iPhone unless you share it yourself.

What the app stores, on your iPhone only

  • Battery readings (charge, voltage, current, power, cell voltages, temperatures and similar values), saved every 10 seconds while connected and kept for 30 days in a file inside the app.
  • The identifier of the battery you connected to, so the app can reconnect to it on its own.

This data is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. It is removed when you tap "Clear history" in the app or delete the app. It may be included in your own iPhone backups (iCloud or computer), which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No connection to the internet or to any server of the developer. The only network use is Apple's App Store for the free trial and the Pro purchase (see below).
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party code that collects data.
  • No location, contacts, photos, health data or other personal information is accessed.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to find nearby devices so you can choose your battery, and then to read values from it. While connected, it keeps reading in the background so your history is complete. The list of nearby devices is shown to you and not stored.

Free trial and Pro purchase

The 7-day free trial and the one-time Pro unlock are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Payment details, your Apple ID and the purchase record are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The app only asks the App Store whether this Apple ID has started the trial or bought Pro, to know which features to open. The developer does not receive your name, email or payment information; Apple provides only anonymous sales totals.

Sharing

If you use "Export CSV", the app creates a file with your readings and opens the iOS share sheet. Where that file goes is up to you.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
