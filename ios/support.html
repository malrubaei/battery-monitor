Battery BMS

Support for the iPhone app

Connecting

  1. Turn on Bluetooth on your iPhone.
  2. Close any other app or web page connected to the battery: it accepts only one connection at a time.
  3. Open the app, tap Connect and choose the device named BMS-…. If no device has that name, try the unnamed device with the strongest signal.

Which batteries work?

Batteries whose management board (BMS) uses the Nations BLE UART Bluetooth module with the Modbus protocol. Not every BMS uses it. Tap Show example in the app to see what it looks like with sample data.

Recording in the background

Once connected, the app records a reading every 10 seconds, also while the phone is locked, and keeps 30 days of history. Recording stops if you close the app by swiping it away in the app switcher; open the app again to resume. Low Power Mode can make iOS pause it more often.

Free trial and Pro

Live readings, background recording and the last hour of charts are free. Pro adds charts for 6 hours up to 30 days and CSV export. You can try Pro free for 7 days (Charts tab → Try Pro free for 7 days); nothing is charged when the trial ends, the Pro features just lock again. Pro is then a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

New phone or reinstalled the app? Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases with the same Apple ID. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com; purchases are handled by Apple.
