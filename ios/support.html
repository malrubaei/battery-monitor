Battery BMS

Help and support for the iPhone app

Connecting the first time

  1. Turn on Bluetooth on your iPhone.
  2. Close any other app or web page connected to the battery: it accepts only one connection at a time.
  3. Open the app and tap Connect. Choose the device named BMS-…. If no device has that name, tap Battery not in the list and pick the unnamed device or the one with the strongest signal.
  4. Allow Bluetooth when iOS asks.

After that the app remembers the battery and reconnects on its own. To look around without a battery, tap Show example.

More than one battery

Tap the gear at the top right of the Now screen to open Settings. Under Batteries:

  • Add battery finds another battery nearby.
  • Tap a battery to rename it, connect, disconnect or forget it. The name shows at the top of the Now screen.
  • The app talks to one battery at a time; connecting to another disconnects the current one. Forgetting a battery keeps its recorded history.

Settings also has the theme: Match iPhone, Light or Dark.

Which batteries work?

Batteries whose management board (BMS) uses the Nations BLE UART Bluetooth module with the Modbus protocol. Not every BMS uses it. Tap Show example in the app to see what it looks like with sample data.

Recording in the background

While connected, the app records a reading every 10 seconds, also while the phone is locked, and keeps 30 days of history. Recording stops if you swipe the app away in the app switcher, turn off Bluetooth or the battery is out of range; open the app again to resume. Low Power Mode can make iOS pause it more often, which leaves gaps in the charts.

Price

The app is completely free. Every feature, including all chart periods and CSV export, is open to everyone. There is no trial, no in-app purchase, no subscription and no ads.

Common problems

  • The battery is not in the list. Close other apps or the web page connected to it, move closer, then tap Battery not in the list.
  • "Turn on Bluetooth to connect". Turn on Bluetooth in Control Center.
  • Bluetooth was denied. iPhone Settings → Battery BMS → turn on Bluetooth.
  • "This battery isn't known to the iPhone any more". Forget it in Settings and add it again.
  • Connected but no readings. The board probably does not use the supported module.
  • Deleting my data. Charts → Clear history, or delete the app. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
