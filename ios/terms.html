What the app is for

The app shows and records readings from compatible battery management boards (BMS) over Bluetooth. The readings come from the battery's own board and are shown for information only. They may be delayed, incomplete or wrong. Do not rely on the app for safety decisions; always follow the battery maker's instructions for charging, storage and use. The app only reads values; it does not change settings on the battery.

Compatibility

The app works only with boards that use the Nations BLE UART Bluetooth module with the Modbus protocol. Use Show example in the app to see it before you buy anything.
